# OctOpus — 3-Minute Investor Pitch Script

~430 words · ~140 wpm · timing cues per slide. Lines in *italics* are stage directions, not spoken.

---

## Slide 1 · Hero — 0:00–0:20

Hi, I'm Doudou, founder of OctOpus.

Imagine telling an AI: "Connect my data warehouse. Clean my sales data. Build a dashboard. Forecast next quarter's revenue." And it just... does it. All of it.

That's OctOpus — an AI data scientist for the enterprise. Not a copilot. The whole job.

## Slide 2 · Problem — 0:20–0:40

Every company sits on data and needs predictions — but getting from a raw warehouse to a decision still means SQL, notebooks, MLOps, and a data scientist who's scarce, expensive, and already stretched thin.

So most teams just... don't. Decisions that need a model get made on gut feel.

## Slide 3 · Product — 0:40–1:00

OctOpus closes that whole loop. It thinks, tries, checks, and fixes — like a real data scientist. You talk to it in plain language; it connects to Databricks, Snowflake, BigQuery, or a file, and comes back with a clean answer, a live dashboard, and a deployed forecasting model. One conversation.

## Slide 4 · Demo — 1:00–1:15

This isn't a concept. *(gesture at demo)* A customer gave it their data at night. I went to bed. It went to work. By morning: analysis done, model trained, report filled in.

## Slide 5 · Traction — 1:15–1:40

Built nights and weekends, with a day job — and it's already real: $8.2K monthly revenue, 13 self-serve paying customers, 9 enterprise PoCs across logistics, oil & gas, and SaaS — 4 already converted to paid. And inbound conversations with IBM, Volvo Group, SKF, Ericsson, and AstraZeneca. No outbound. They came to us.

## Slide 6 · Moat — 1:40–2:00

Claude and Cursor can write code — that's not the moat. The moat is knowing what to test, what to trust, and what to ship for a specific forecasting problem. OctOpus compounds that domain reasoning on every run, and delivers in hours, not weeks.

## Slide 7 · Market — 2:00–2:15

This is a $300 billion market, because every team that decides with data is a customer. 90 million knowledge workers touch data daily — 1% of them at our price points is multi-billion ARR.

## Slide 8 · Business model — 2:15–2:25

We land at $20 a month self-serve, expand to team plans, and grow into enterprise contracts — moving toward pay-per-result.

## Slide 9 · Founder — 2:25–2:40

Why me? PhD in machine learning. Built production AI at Maersk used in 130+ countries — systems where an outage cost $50K in hours. I built the product, the traction, and the first sales alone.

## Slide 10 · Ask — 2:40–3:00

I'm raising $500K pre-seed: to go full-time, hire exceptional sales and engineering, and win the US and Europe. The category leader in AI-native data science gets decided in the next two years.

I intend for it to be OctOpus. Thank you.

---

### Delivery notes

- **Pace:** 140 wpm feels calm; don't rush the traction slide — the numbers do the work.
- **Pauses:** after "The whole job." (slide 1) and "They came to us." (slide 5) — let both land.
- **If running over:** cut slide 8 to one sentence ("$20 a month to enterprise contracts") — pricing is in the deck.
- **Q&A pivots:** competition → slide 6 matrix; sizing → slide 7 bottom-up math; solo founder → slide 9 "100% owner, open to exceptional hires."
